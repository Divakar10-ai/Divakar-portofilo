# DIVAKAR — Cinematic Portfolio

A cinematic personal portfolio created for **Divakar — Artist · Creative · Storyteller**.

The portfolio combines WebGL, interactive visuals, animation, photography, creative projects and storytelling into one immersive web experience.

## Scenes

**Scene 1 — The Opening**

Black screen → the creator walks out of the dark → the DIVAKAR wordmark materialises behind him → chips, arrows and dots snap into place → the header draws itself in → the composition settles and breathes.

**Scene 2 — The Creative Universe**

A cinematic visual universe where the visitor scrolls into an immersive experience. Cards, particles, lighting, movement and interactive elements come together to create a living creative space.

**Scene 3 — The Time Machine**

A warm black room → the timeline rail draws itself → a giant clock resolves → six year cards arrive → the floor mechanism turns → and the visitor takes control.

Moving the cursor across the timeline travels through different years and highlights the selected moment.

**Scene 4 — Projects**

An interactive project universe showcasing creative work through animated cards, cinematic lighting, depth, movement and responsive layouts.

**Finale — The Closing**

The portfolio ends with a cinematic closing sequence combining imagery, typography, atmospheric lighting, motion and personal messaging.
